Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026

social-autopost ("the App") is a personal tool operated by Abdul Rahman. It runs only on the operator's own computer. It publishes the operator's own videos to the operator's own accounts on TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn. The App is not offered to the public and has no other users.

1. Information the App accesses

When the operator connects an account, the App receives only what is needed to publish videos:

  • Account identifiers and basic profile information: for example, a TikTok open ID, display name and avatar (scope user.info.basic), a LinkedIn member ID, a Facebook Page ID and an Instagram account ID.
  • Access tokens issued by each platform through its official sign-in (OAuth).
  • Content the operator chooses to publish: video files, titles, captions and descriptions.

The App does not collect followers, messages, comments, analytics, contacts or any information about other people.

2. How the information is used

The information is used only to upload the operator's videos and post text to the operator's own accounts. For example, it uploads videos to the operator's TikTok drafts (scope video.upload), where the operator reviews and publishes them in the TikTok app.

3. Storage and security

Tokens and settings are stored only in local files on the operator's computer. They are not uploaded to any server run by the App, because the App has none. Video files stay on the operator's computer until they are uploaded directly to the platform the operator chose.

4. Sharing

The App does not sell, rent or share data with anyone. Data is sent only to the platform the operator is publishing to (TikTok, Google/YouTube, Meta or LinkedIn), through that platform's official API.

5. YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time at Google security settings.

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the account is connected. To remove the App's access:

  • Delete the App's local token files, and
  • Revoke access in each platform's settings: TikTok (Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services), Google (link above), Facebook/Instagram (Settings > Apps and websites / Business integrations), and LinkedIn (Settings > Data privacy > Permitted services).

7. Children

The App is not directed at children and is not used by anyone under 18.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
